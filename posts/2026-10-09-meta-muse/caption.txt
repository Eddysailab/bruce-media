Meta Muse for Small Business: what Black-owned businesses should know before letting AI run their marketing.

On September 29, Meta launched Muse, a free AI agent for small businesses in the US and Canada. It connects to your Facebook Page, Instagram, ad account and tools like Shopify, Stripe and Canva, then drafts posts, emails and campaigns in the background. Nothing goes live without your approval.

Here's the part most people will skip. Muse learns your brand voice before it writes a single word. If your logo, colors, fonts and website don't tell a clear story yet, AI will repeat that confusion at scale.
