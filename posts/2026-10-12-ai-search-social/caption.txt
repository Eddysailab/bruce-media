AI search and social media checklist: 4 things Black-owned businesses should tighten up now that Google's AI answers cite social posts.

A new SE Ranking study of 100,000 keywords, covered by Social Media Today on October 7, found social platform links in 67.66% of Google AI Overview responses and 32.39% of Google AI Mode replies. ChatGPT was far lower at 8.4%. In AI Mode, social sources appeared in 48.58% of non-branded searches, the ones where people look for a category and haven't picked a business yet.

Here's what to tighten up:
1. The same business name, logo and colors on every profile.
2. A bio that says exactly what you do and where you serve.
3. Posts that answer the questions your customers actually ask.
4. A website that matches your socials and backs up what they say.

There's no one-size-fits-all formula for AI visibility. A clear, consistent brand makes you easier to recognize wherever you get cited.
